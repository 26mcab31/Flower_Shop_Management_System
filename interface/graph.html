Breadth First Search (BFS)

Select a starting location and click "Run BFS".

How BFS Works

BFS uses a Queue to visit locations level by level.

  • Step 1: Select a starting location.
  • Step 2: Mark the location as visited.
  • Step 3: Add it to the queue.
  • Step 4: Remove one location from the queue.
  • Step 5: Visit its unvisited neighbours.
  • Step 6: Continue until the queue is empty.
